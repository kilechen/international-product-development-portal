A. Customer Requirements

Advanced Repair Night Cream

  • Target melasma, dark spots, uneven skin tone and signs of premature aging.
  • Support overnight repair and the skin barrier.
  • Gentle positioning for sensitive skin; minimize irritation, burning, itching and redness.
  • Non-comedogenic direction with sebum and pore-appearance care.
  • Lightweight and non-greasy texture suitable for hot and humid weather.
  • Hero ingredients must support future brand positioning and ingredient education.

Advanced Protective Day Cream

  • Target SPF50+ / PA++++ with UVA / UVB protection.
  • HEV / blue-light protection direction and daytime antioxidant support.
  • Designed for Pakistan's extreme heat, humidity and perspiration.
  • Breathable, lightweight, dry-touch and non-greasy finish.
  • Sweat-resistant direction without a heavy “mask” feeling or obvious melting sensation.
  • Non-comedogenic direction with sebum and pore-appearance care.
Branding requirement: Beauzyn would like the final Hero / Key Active Ingredients to be confirmed before development progresses too far, because these ingredients will be used for brand positioning, dermatologist communication and market education in Pakistan.

B. IMEI Technical Notes

Formulation approach

  • IMEI R&D will evaluate all requirements as one integrated formulation system rather than simply combining individual claims.
  • Some targets involve technical trade-offs, especially high UV protection, sweat resistance, lightweight skin feel, barrier comfort and non-comedogenic positioning.
  • We will recommend the best balance for performance, stability, skin feel and the Pakistan climate.

Hero ingredient evaluation

  • Technical feasibility and formulation compatibility.
  • Recommended effective use level and safety considerations.
  • Raw-material supply stability, MOQ and lead time.
  • Evidence / technical support available from the supplier.
  • Differentiation and marketing value for the Pakistan market.

Current ingredient directions under evaluation

Night Cream:
Snow Peptide 360 · Tranexamic Acid · Salmon-derived repair ingredient · Antarctic Marine Glycoprotein · additional brightening / barrier-support actives after R&D review.
Day Cream:
Hydroxyphenoxy Propionic Acid + Ethyl Vitamin C brightening concept · Uvinul A Plus · Tinosorb S · Tinosorb M · additional antioxidant / barrier / thermal-stress support after R&D review.
Testing note: At the current sample-development stage, SPF / PA performance will be evaluated using instrument-based testing. Final claims and commercial testing requirements will be confirmed separately before launch.

A. 客戶需求

Advanced Repair Night Cream 修護晚霜

  • 改善肝斑、黑斑、膚色不均與早期老化跡象。
  • 支援夜間修護與肌膚屏障維持。
  • 以敏感肌可接受的溫和方向設計，降低刺激、灼熱、搔癢與泛紅風險。
  • 以不致粉刺方向設計，並兼顧油脂與毛孔外觀管理。
  • 適合高溫高濕氣候的輕盈、不油膩質地。
  • 核心成分需能支援未來品牌定位與成分教育。

Advanced Protective Day Cream 防護日霜

  • 目標 SPF50+ / PA++++，提供 UVA / UVB 防護。
  • HEV／藍光防護方向與日間抗氧化支持。
  • 針對巴基斯坦極端高溫、高濕與大量流汗環境設計。
  • 透氣、輕盈、乾爽、不油膩膚感。
  • 朝抗汗方向設計，避免厚重面膜感或明顯融化感。
  • 以不致粉刺方向設計，並兼顧油脂與毛孔外觀管理。
品牌需求： Beauzyn 希望在配方開發進一步推進前，先確認最終 Hero / Key Active Ingredients，因為這些核心成分將用於巴基斯坦市場的品牌定位、皮膚科醫師溝通與市場教育。

B. IMEI 說明

配方設計原則

  • IMEI 研發團隊會將所有需求視為一個整體配方系統進行評估，而非單純把各個訴求疊加。
  • 部分目標之間存在技術取捨，尤其是高防曬力、抗汗、輕盈膚感、屏障舒適度與不致粉刺方向。
  • 我們將針對功效、安定性、膚感與巴基斯坦氣候條件取得最佳平衡。

核心成分評估原則

  • 技術可行性與配方相容性。
  • 建議有效添加量與安全性考量。
  • 原料供應穩定性、MOQ 與交期。
  • 原料商可提供的技術／功效支持資料。
  • 巴基斯坦市場的差異化與行銷價值。

目前評估中的成分方向

晚霜：
Snow Peptide 360 · Tranexamic Acid 傳明酸 · Salmon-derived repair ingredient 鮭魚來源修護成分 · Antarctic Marine Glycoprotein 南極海洋糖蛋白 · 以及研發評估後適合的亮白／屏障修護成分。
日霜：
Hydroxyphenoxy Propionic Acid + Ethyl Vitamin C 亮白概念 · Uvinul A Plus · Tinosorb S · Tinosorb M · 以及研發評估後適合的抗氧化／屏障／熱壓力防護成分。
測試說明：目前樣品開發階段，SPF / PA 將先採儀器測試方式進行評估。正式上市前之最終宣稱與商業測試需求，將另行確認。
